Privacy Policy for Commit

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Commit ("Commit", "we", "us", or "our") is a digital wellbeing and app-blocking application designed to help users reduce distractions and stay focused.

This Privacy Policy explains how Commit accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Commit application.

1. Developer and Contact Information

App: Commit

Privacy contact: adityadhadwal.5@gmail.com

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, contact us at the email address above.

2. No User Account Required

Commit does not require you to create an account using your name, email address, phone number, password, or social-media account.

Commit uses an anonymous installation identity so that the application can synchronize challenge information with its backend services and support continuity across supported reinstall scenarios.

3. Information Commit Processes

Depending on how you use Commit, the application may process the following information.

3.1 Anonymous Installation Information

Commit creates a random installation identifier for your installation. A corresponding cryptographic representation of the installation credential is stored by our backend for authentication and security.

Commit may also use a cryptographic hash derived from the Android installation identifier available on your device to help recognize a supported reinstall. The raw Android installation identifier is not sent to our backend.

3.2 Challenge Information

When you create a challenge, Commit may process information such as:

  • Challenge start and end timestamps
  • Challenge duration
  • Challenge status
  • Apps selected by you for blocking
  • Selected app package identifiers
  • Challenge events required to maintain challenge state
  • Emergency-access events

This information is used to provide the core challenge and app-blocking functionality.

3.3 Device and App Usage Information

Commit uses Android system capabilities to determine which application is currently in use and to apply the blocking rules you configure.

Commit may use Android Accessibility Service and Usage Access functionality for this purpose.

These capabilities are used to provide the app-blocking functionality. Commit does not use Accessibility Service to read the contents of messages, passwords, financial information, or other screen content for advertising or profiling purposes.

3.4 Network and Security Information

When Commit communicates with its backend services, technical information associated with the network request may be processed by the infrastructure provider, such as network and security information needed to deliver the service, protect the service, and enforce rate limits.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by Commit is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide app-blocking and digital wellbeing functionality
  • To create and maintain challenges
  • To synchronize challenge state with our backend
  • To maintain challenge continuity when supported
  • To detect and record challenge interruptions or important challenge events
  • To provide emergency-access functionality
  • To protect the service against abuse and unauthorized requests
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of Commit

We do not use the information described in this Privacy Policy to serve personalized advertising.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Commit does not require or intentionally collect the following for normal app use:

  • Your name
  • Your email address as an in-app account identifier
  • Your phone number
  • Your password
  • Your contacts
  • Your precise location
  • Your photos or videos
  • Your microphone recordings
  • Your financial or payment information

The privacy contact email listed in this policy is provided for communication with the developer and is not required to create a Commit user account.

6. Sharing of Information

Commit does not sell your personal information.

Commit may transmit information to service providers that operate infrastructure required to provide the application. For example, Commit uses Supabase-hosted backend infrastructure for database and server-side functionality.

Information transmitted to our backend is limited to information necessary for the application's functionality, security, and synchronization.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect the security of the service, or prevent fraud or abuse.

7. Data Security

Commit uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information processed by the application and backend services.

Network communication between the application and backend services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Sensitive credentials stored locally by the application are protected using Android's secure storage mechanisms.

Backend access is protected using server-side authentication, request signing, access controls, and rate limiting.

No security measure can guarantee absolute security. We continuously work to improve the security and reliability of Commit.

8. Data Retention

We retain backend information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Commit's functionality, maintain security, prevent abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Challenge and installation information may remain on backend systems while it is required to maintain the service and its security features.

Local application data may remain on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, or uninstall Commit, subject to Android system behavior.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Commit does not currently provide user accounts. Therefore, the Google Play account-deletion requirement applicable to apps that create user accounts does not apply to Commit's accountless design.

10. Children's Privacy

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.
